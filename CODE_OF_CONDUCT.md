# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, in full. The complete text, which is the binding version of these rules, is published at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## In short

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project, whatever their experience, background, identity or language, without harassment. The Covenant asks all participants to:

- be welcoming, respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback gracefully; take responsibility for mistakes;
- focus on what is best for the community, not only for oneself;
- not use sexualised language or imagery, not attack anyone personally or politically, not troll, insult or harass anyone, and not publish other people's private information (such as an address, or a recording or transcript of a conversation) without their explicit permission.

This project handles recordings of other people, so one more point is specific to us: please never post audio, transcripts or notes of real meetings in issues, pull requests or discussions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Scope

The Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them. They follow the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, in proportion to the harm.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately: open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and put "Conduct report" in the title. Only the maintainers can see such a report. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person who reports is respected.
