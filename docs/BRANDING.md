# Branding: the names, the icon and the optional "built by" line

Three small things you may want to change when you build Catch Everything (CATE) for yourself or for your team: the names of the app, the app icon, and a line that says who built a copy. None of them changes what the app records, stores or sends.

## The names

The product is called **Catch Everything**, short name **CATE**. Both are build inputs, so a copy that is shipped under another name needs no change in the sources: `MCAP_APP_NAME` is the human name (default `Catch Everything`: the display name, the usage strings of `Info.plist`, every message, menu item and notification of the app, the volume of the disk image) and `MCAP_APP_SHORT_NAME` is the file-safe short name (default `CATE`: `CATE.app`, its program, the disk image and the release zip). The rules and what keeps its internal name (the data folder, the log folder and the Keychain items stay `MeetingCapture`) are in [CONFIGURATION.md](CONFIGURATION.md#names-and-identifier-of-the-app). The icon below carries no text, so it needs no change for a new name.

## The app icon

`app/Resources/AppIcon.icns` is generated by `scripts/make_icon.swift` (CoreGraphics and the `iconutil` that ships with macOS; no other tool, no network). The concept is a rounded square on the macOS icon grid with a **notes-and-pen glyph**: the icon says "notes", not "audio", and it no longer relies on a waveform on a rounded square, which comes close to the look of Apple's Voice Memos icon. The shipped icon is variant `D1`.

| Variant | Glyph and mood |
|---|---|
| `D1` | a clipboard with four written lines and a pencil, on deep indigo glass: blue-to-indigo gradient, soft light from the top left, glass sheen (the shipped icon) |
| `D2` | a speech bubble with three lines of text and a small pencil, on teal-to-blue glass |
| `D3` | a spiral notebook page with a handwriting scribble and a small red recording dot, on violet glass |
| `A` | the former icon: a white-to-ice audio waveform and a red recording dot on deep indigo glass |
| `B` | the former waveform on bright blue, clean: lighter and more saturated, no sheen |
| `C` | the former waveform in blue on graphite |

All of them have an inner rim, a gentle drop shadow that stays inside the icon grid and a soft shadow under the paper and the pencil. `A`, `B` and `C` are kept under their flags and render byte for byte as before.

```bash
swiftc -O scripts/make_icon.swift -o /tmp/make_icon
/tmp/make_icon --variant D2 --png /tmp/icon-D2.png        # one 512 px preview to look at (--size N for another size)
/tmp/make_icon app/Resources/AppIcon.icns --variant D1    # bake a variant into the app (D1 is the default)
/tmp/make_icon /tmp/x.icns --keep-iconset /tmp/x.iconset  # also keep the ten PNGs of the iconset
```

- Every size is drawn at its own pixel size, not scaled down from one big picture. The 16 and 32 px sizes are a compact version: whole-pixel edges, paper and lines on the pixel grid (one-pixel lines every second row at 16 px, two-pixel lines at 32 px), a thick pencil, a bigger dot, and none of the soft effects (shadow, halo, sheen), so they stay legible in System Settings lists, in notifications and in Finder lists. At 16 px the glyph is a small white page with lines and an orange pencil: it reads as notes, not as detail.
- The output is deterministic: the same variant gives the same bytes on every run (`D1` was baked twice and compared).
- `scripts/build_app.sh` copies the `.icns` into the app before signing and refuses to build when it does not decode with `iconutil`. After `make install` it also asks Launch Services to re-read the installed app (this app only), so the new icon shows up without a login; with `make build` only, Finder may keep showing the old one until you log out and in.

## The menu-bar icons

The menu-bar icon is an SF Symbol that follows the state of the app (`StatusMenuController.menuIcon`, `app/Sources/StatusMenu.swift`, one pure function with tests):

| State | Symbol (first choice) | Fallbacks, in this order |
|---|---|---|
| idle, waiting for a meeting | `pencil.and.list.clipboard` | `list.clipboard`, `waveform` |
| a meeting was detected | the idle symbol (SF Symbols has no circle variant of it) | the same |
| recording | red `pencil.and.scribble`, alternating each second with `pencil.line` | `pencil`, `record.circle.fill` |
| paused | `pause.circle` | `waveform` |
| automatic detection off | `pencil.slash` (the clipboard has no slashed variant) | `waveform.slash`, `waveform` |
| processing | `ellipsis.bubble` | `ellipsis.circle`, `gearshape` |
| done | `checkmark.circle` | `checkmark` |
| error | `exclamationmark.triangle` | - |

The two frames of the recording icon follow the parity of the elapsed seconds of the ticker that already runs once a second while recording, and stay on the first frame when **Reduce Motion** is on. Every name was checked three ways: it renders as `NSImage(systemSymbolName:)` on the Mac used for the check (a scratch program drew them on white and on dark), it is listed in `/System/Library/CoreServices/CoreGlyphs.bundle/Contents/Resources/name_availability.plist` with a release at or below macOS 14.4 (`pencil.and.list.clipboard` and `pencil.and.scribble` are SF Symbols 5 / macOS 14.0, `pencil.line` and `list.clipboard` 13.0, the others older), and the self test (`selftest-session`, S-ST-01) repeats the render and the availability check on whatever Mac runs it. **Not verified:** nobody ran the app on macOS 14.4 itself; the list says which symbols are older than that.

## "Built by": naming who built a copy

The open-source tree names nobody. An internal build can say who built it:

```bash
MCAP_BUILT_BY="Example Team, example.org" make install ARGS="--python --with-silero --pre-download-model"
```

`make build` is incremental and does not notice a changed `MCAP_BUILT_BY`; use `MCAP_BUILT_BY="..." make clean build` there.

With the variable set, `scripts/build_app.sh`:

- adds the key `MCBuiltBy` to the app's `Info.plist`, and turns `NSHumanReadableCopyright` into "Copyright 2026 Meeting Capture contributors. Built by &lt;value&gt;" (Finder's Get Info shows it);
- the menu-bar menu item **About Catch Everything** (the human name of the build) then shows the standard About panel with the version and the line "Built by &lt;value&gt;".

The copyright holder line, "Copyright 2026 Meeting Capture contributors", keeps the project's earlier name on purpose: it names the people who contributed to this source repository, not the product, and it is the same words in `NOTICE`, both READMEs, `Info.plist` and the About panel. Renaming the product did not rename the holder.

Without the variable (the default), nothing about a builder is in the bundle: there is no `MCBuiltBy`, the copyright line is the neutral "Copyright 2026 Meeting Capture contributors", and the About panel shows the version only. The script checks this on every build.

Rules for the value (the same in `scripts/build_app.sh` and in `StatusMenuController.normalizedBuiltBy`, `app/Sources/StatusMenu.swift`; both are tested):

- one line: newlines, tabs, other control characters and runs of spaces become one space, and leading and trailing spaces are dropped;
- at most 80 characters (counted in characters, so Korean or accented names are not cut in the middle); a longer value is cut and ends in `…`;
- any text is fine, including `&`, `<`, `>` and quotes (the plist is escaped by `plutil`); it must be valid UTF-8, or the build stops before compiling.

The About panel shows the version string only. There is no build date or build time anywhere, on purpose: the same sources and the same `MCAP_BUILT_BY` give the same app, which keeps builds deterministic. Do not hard-code a person's or a company's name in the sources, the template or the scripts; pass it at build time. If you distribute builds to other people, also change the bundle identifier and the names as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#forks-that-distribute-builds), and keep `MCAP_BUILT_BY` out of the environment of `scripts/release.sh` if you want the public release to stay neutral.

To see what a built copy says: `plutil -p ~/Applications/CATE.app/Contents/Info.plist | grep -E "MCBuiltBy|Copyright"`.
